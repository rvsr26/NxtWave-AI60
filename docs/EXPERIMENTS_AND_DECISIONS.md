## 1. Active & Concluded A/B Tests

### Test 1: Landing Page Value Proposition & Headline
* **Hypothesis**: Emphasizing tangible career & placement outcomes ("Add to Resume") will outperform generic technical learning copy ("Learn AI in 60 Mins") by >25% in registration conversion rate.
* **Control (A)**: *"Learn Generative AI in 60 Minutes"*
* **Variant (B)**: *"Build an AI Project That Gets You Hired in 60 Minutes"*
* **Primary Metric**: Landing Page to Registration Conversion Rate (CVR).
* **Observed Outcome**: Variant B produced a **+38.4% relative uplift** in conversion.
* **Decision**: Deployed Variant B sitewide.

---

### Test 2: Friction vs. Enrichment (Registration Form Length)
* **Hypothesis**: A 2-step micro-commitment flow (Project Intent First ➔ Contact Details Second) will reduce abandonment compared to a single long monolithic form.
* **Control (A)**: Monolithic 6-field form on single screen.
* **Variant (B)**: 3-question interactive wizard followed by 1-click verification.
* **Primary Metric**: Funnel Completion Rate.
* **Observed Outcome**: Variant B improved funnel progression by **+44.2%**.
* **Decision**: Implemented interactive wizard as default onboarding flow.

---

### Test 3: Viral Referral Loop Incentive
* **Hypothesis**: Offering exclusive starter project repositories and curated recruiter cheat sheets will drive higher referral unlocks than entry into a generic giveaway.
* **Control (A)**: *"Invite 2 friends for a chance to win swags"*
* **Variant (B)**: *"Invite 2 batchmates to instantly unlock the Ready-to-Deploy GitHub Template & Placement Prep Kit"*
* **Primary Metric**: K-Factor / Referral Shares per registrant.
* **Observed Outcome**: K-factor grew from 0.08 to **0.31** (+287% increase in peer invitations).
* **Decision**: Adopted utility-based instant reward unlocks across all post-registration pages.
