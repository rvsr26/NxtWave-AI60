# 🚀 AI60: Growth Plan & Strategy Document
**Challenge**: NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge  
**Campaign**: "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"  
**Goal**: 500 Verified Final-Year Engineering Student Registrations  
**Budget**: ₹2,000 INR  
**Duration**: 7 Days (Simulation)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Hypothesis
Final-year engineering students face acute placement anxiety and need tangible proof of skills on their resumes and GitHub profiles. Generic workshop marketing fails because students perceive webinars as passive lectures with zero career ROI. 

**Our Winning Growth Thesis:**
> Shift from selling a "lecture" to giving them an immediate personal utility: **The AI Project Passport™** — a customized, branch-specific AI project specification generated before they even register, turning curiosity and self-interest into a high-converting registration engine.

---

## 2. Target Persona & Value Proposition
* **Primary Persona**: Final-year engineering students (CS, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil) preparing for campus placements.
* **Core Pain Points**: 
  - Resume lacks modern generative AI projects.
  - Overwhelmed by tutorials; don't know where to start without GPUs or deep math.
  - High friction and fear of failure.
* **The Hook**: "Build and deploy a functional, resume-worthy AI project in 60 minutes flat — zero prior AI experience required."

## 5. Experimentation & Growth Loops
1. **The Passport Loop**: Visitor selects domain & branch ➔ Generates AI Project Passport ➔ Unlocks GitHub repo template upon completing registration ➔ Shares milestone badge.
2. **Campus League**: Real-time competition among colleges (e.g., Amrita, SRM, VIT, JNTU) driving organic social proof.
3. **Double-Sided WhatsApp Forwarding**: Captions engineered for placement WhatsApp groups focusing on "placement project prep".
